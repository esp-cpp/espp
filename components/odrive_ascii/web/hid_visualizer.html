WebHID is not available in this browser. WebHID (navigator.hid) requires a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave — Firefox and Safari do not implement it) served over https://, http://localhost, or a file:// URL.

Sticks & Hat

Generic-Desktop X/Y & Rx/Ry axes render as 2D sticks; hat switches render as a d-pad.

Axes

Z / Rz / sliders and any other axes render as bars (normalized by their logical min/max).
