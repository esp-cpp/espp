BNO055 9-Axis Absolute Orientation IMU
**************************************

The `Bno055` component provides a driver for the Bosch BNO055 9-axis absolute
orientation sensor. The BNO055 runs sensor fusion on its own microcontroller
and reports orientation, linear acceleration, and gravity alongside the raw
accelerometer, gyroscope, and magnetometer data.
