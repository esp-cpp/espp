WDI (Wheelchair Digital Interface) APIs
***************************************

.. toctree::
    :maxdepth: 1

    wdi

The ``wdi`` component implements the `Open-Mobility-Hub Wheelchair HID
<https://open-mobility-hub.github.io/wheelchair-digital-interface/>`_
specification (v3.2) — a standard bidirectional interface between a powered
wheelchair and an app / accessory over **USB** or **Bluetooth LE**, in both the
**device** (accessory) and **host** (wheelchair) roles.
