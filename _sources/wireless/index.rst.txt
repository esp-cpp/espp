Wireless & Radio APIs
*********************

.. toctree::
    :maxdepth: 1

    sx126x
    gps
    meshtastic

These components provide sub-GHz LoRa radio, GNSS/GPS, and Meshtastic-compatible
mesh networking support. They are used together on LoRa-capable boards such as
the LilyGo T-Deck and the M5Stack Cardputer-Adv (with the LoRa+GPS Cap).
